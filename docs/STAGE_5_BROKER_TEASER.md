# Stage 5: Broker Teaser (Marketplace Listing)

## Purpose
The public-facing sales document for charter brokers and marketplace listings. Creates desire without revealing the full journey. Must be compelling enough to generate inquiries while protecting the proprietary experience design.

---

## When Generated
- **Trigger:** Experience creation (always generated with Stage 1)
- **Timing:** Immediate, alongside Stage 1
- **Access:** Public — brokers, marketplace, website

---

### 5.4 Emotional Description

**Purpose:** Paint the picture of what's possible — the transformation, not the logistics.

**Length:** 200-300 words

**Structure:**
1. What this is (1-2 sentences)
2. What this is NOT (differentiation)
3. The approach/method (high-level)
4. The outcome/feeling
5. Memorable closing line

**Example (Phoenix Rising):**
```
THE EXPERIENCE
What Awaits

Phoenix Rising™ is an eight-day wellness voyage along the French Riviera, 
designed for high achievers who have given everything to the world — and 
are ready to reclaim what's theirs.

This is not a spa retreat. It's not a digital detox with yoga and smoothies. 
It's a carefully choreographed restoration journey that meets you where you 
actually are: successful, driven, and running on empty.

The journey begins by celebrating your arrival — you made it, you're here, 
breathe. Then it gently guides you through stillness, into honest confrontation 
with what you've been sacrificing, and finally into the steady rise back to 
full power.

World-class spas become temples of remembrance. Cutting-edge longevity science 
meets ancient healing wisdom. The Mediterranean becomes your mirror — reflecting 
back not who you've had to be, but who you actually are when no one needs 
anything from you.

By the time you disembark, you won't be a different person. You'll be yourself 
— restored to full capacity, unshakeable from within, at peace with everything 
you've built. With nothing left to prove to anyone. Including yourself.

The phoenix doesn't transform into something new. It burns away everything 
that isn't essential — and rises as what it always was.
```

### 5.5 Signature Highlights

**Purpose:** Tantalizing glimpses that create intrigue. NOT a day-by-day reveal.

**Format:** 5-7 items with title + 1-2 sentence description

**Rules:**
- Never reveal full structure
- Focus on emotional outcomes
- Include at least one legendary POI reference
- Include the takeaway/kit
- Mix experiential and emotional

**Example (Phoenix Rising):**
```
SIGNATURE HIGHLIGHTS
A Glimpse of What's Possible

◈ Pre-Voyage Power Profiling
  Before you board, a confidential consultation maps your stress patterns 
  and restoration goals. Your journey is calibrated to you.

◈ The Riviera's Most Legendary Sanctuaries
  Hotel du Cap-Eden-Roc. Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo. Cheval Blanc St-Tropez. 
  Pilgrimages to places where the world's most powerful have come to 
  remember themselves.

◈ The Nietzsche Path
  Walk the clifftop trail where the philosopher conceived his vision of 
  eternal return — not power over others, but power over oneself.

◈ Precision Longevity Protocols
  IV therapy, NAD+, cryotherapy, thermal circuits — your cells receiving 
  what they've been requesting.

◈ The Stillpoint
  There is a day — the sacred pause — where transformation becomes possible. 
  Not through doing, but through finally stopping.

◈ Memory Anchors
  Each day is designed around a single powerful phrase. When life gets 
  loud again, these anchors bring you back.

◈ The Phoenix Rising™ Kit
  Sleep ritual elements, guided meditation, and your written Experience 
  Script. The fire becomes portable.
```

### 5.6 Ideal Guest Profile

**Purpose:** Help brokers identify the right clients. Enable self-selection.

**Format:** "This voyage is for those who..." + 5-7 criteria

**Example (Phoenix Rising):**
```
THE IDEAL GUEST
This Voyage Is For Those Who...

◆ Have achieved extraordinary success — and are exhausted by the weight 
  of maintaining it
◆ Are tired of being the strong one, the capable one, the one everyone 
  depends on
◆ Know they're running on empty but haven't had permission to stop
◆ Want restoration without sacrificing quality, privacy, or sophistication
◆ Don't need another achievement — they need to feel alive again
◆ Are ready to stop proving and start living
◆ Understand that true power comes from within, not from external validation
```

interface Stage5Output {

  script_id: string;

}

## Neo4j Query for Stage 5 Generation

```cypher
// Get all data needed for Broker Teaser
MATCH (es:experience_script {lexa_uid: $script_id})
MATCH (es)-[:BASED_ON]->(ea:experience_arc)
MATCH (es)-[:FOR_JOURNEY_TYPE]->(jt:journey_type)
OPTIONAL MATCH (ea)-[:RESONATES_WITH]->(ga:guest_archetype)
OPTIONAL MATCH (ea)-[:PRIMARY_THEME]->(pt:theme_category)
RETURN {
  script_id: es.lexa_uid,
  experience_name: es.name,
  custom_tagline: es.custom_tagline,
  region: es.region,
  duration: es.duration_days,
  season: es.season,
  arc: {
    name: ea.name,
    tagline: ea.tagline,
    hook: ea.hook,
    description: ea.description,
    closing_anchor: ea.closing_anchor
  },
  journey_type: jt.name,
  archetypes: collect(DISTINCT {
    name: ga.name,
    core_need: ga.core_need,
    fears: ga.fears,
    desires: ga.desires
  }),
  themes: collect(DISTINCT pt.name)
} AS teaser_data
```

## Generation Priority

Stage 5 should be generated:
1. **Automatically** with every new experience script
2. **First** among all documents (before Stage 2-4)
3. **Stored** for immediate marketplace access
4. **Updated** only when script fundamentally changes

This ensures every experience is marketplace-ready from creation.
