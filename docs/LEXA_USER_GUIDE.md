# LEXA Beta — User Guide for Testers

Welcome to the LEXA beta. You are among the first to experience a new way of designing luxury travel — where the journey is shaped around how you want to feel, not just where you want to go.

This guide walks you through everything you need to test.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Create Your Account

1. Go to the LEXA website (you will receive the link separately)
2. Click **"Begin Your Journey"** or **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your **full name**, email, and a password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Check your email for a verification link and click it
5. You will be redirected to the LEXA chat

**Important:** Use your real name — LEXA uses it to personalise your experience.

---

### 2. Set Your Name (Existing Testers)

If you already have an account but LEXA shows your email instead of your name:

1. Click your name/email in the top-right corner of the chat
2. Go to **Account** > **Edit Profile**
3. Enter your full name and click **Save**
4. Start a new chat — LEXA will now greet you by name

## The Chat Experience

### How It Works

LEXA is not a search engine. It is an experience designer. You tell LEXA what you are craving — in plain language — and LEXA designs a personalised experience script around it.

**The flow is simple:**

1. **You describe what you want** (1 message)
2. **LEXA asks one clarifying question** if needed (where? how long?)
3. **LEXA generates your Experience Script** — a full emotional journey with a hook, description, signature highlights, and target profile

After that, you can refine the script ("make it more intense", "different region", "add wellness") or save it to your account.

### What to Say

LEXA works best with specific, honest input. Here are examples:

**Good (specific):**
- "I want a romantic weekend with my wife in Monaco — lots of wellness and a sunset dinner on a yacht."
- "Adrenaline experiences on the French Riviera. 7 days. I want to feel alive."
- "A restorative escape for two. We are burned out. Somewhere warm, 10 days, no crowds."
- "Family trip with my kids, Greek islands, adventure and culture."

**Also works (less specific — LEXA will ask where/how long):**
- "I need a break. Something special."
- "Culinary journey with my partner."
- "Something for our 25th anniversary."

**Theme cards:** If you are not sure what to say, you can tap one of the theme cards on the left and right side of the chat (visible on desktop). Themes include Romance, Adventure, Wellness, Culinary, Cultural Immersion, and more.

**Voice input:** You can hold the microphone button next to the text input to speak instead of typing. Release to send.

### What You Will Get

After 2-3 messages, LEXA generates an **Experience Script** (Stage 1) containing:

- **Experience Name** — a branded name for your journey (e.g., "Phoenix Rising", "Because of Us")
- **Tagline** — the emotional promise
- **Hook** — 2-3 sentences that should make you feel "they understand me"
- **Description** — 150-200 words painting the transformation (not logistics)
- **Signature Highlights** — 5-7 tantalising glimpses of what awaits
- **Target Profile** — "This voyage is for those who..." (you should recognise yourself)
- **Quick Facts** — duration, region, embarkation, type

### After the Script

Once you see the script, you have these options (buttons will appear):

- **"Perfect — save it"** — saves to your account and generates the full day-by-day journey in the background
- **"More intense"** — LEXA adjusts the script for more adrenaline/adventure
- **"More calm"** — LEXA adjusts toward wellness and restoration
- **"Different region"** — LEXA suggests a different destination

You can also type any feedback: "add a yacht dinner", "remove the hiking", "make it shorter", etc.

## Guest Preferences

### Setting Your Preferences

1. From the chat, click your name in the top-right > **Account**
2. Click **"Guest Preferences"** (gold button)
3. Fill in your preferences across 5 sections:

| Section | What to Set |
|---------|-------------|
| **Dining & Dietary** | Dietary restrictions, allergies, cuisine preferences, drinks |
| **Wellness & Body** | Preferred treatments (massage, yoga, etc.), fitness level, mobility |
| **Cabin & Sleep** | Temperature, pillow type, sleep aids, preferred scents, music |
| **Activities & Pace** | Interests (themes), things to avoid, trip pace |
| **Communication & Privacy** | How you like to be greeted, privacy level, special occasions |

4. Click **Save Preferences**

### How Preferences Are Used

- **In chat:** LEXA knows your preferences and adjusts recommendations accordingly. If you set "wellness-focused" as an interest, LEXA will prioritise spa and restoration POIs.
- **In scripts:** Your preferences influence which signature highlights appear.
- **For crew (future):** When you book a charter, your dietary, wellness, and cabin preferences are automatically included in the crew briefing — no questionnaire needed.

## Your Account

### Account Dashboard

Click your name in the top-right of the chat to access your account. You will see:

- **Membership status** — your current tier
- **Emotional Profile** — what LEXA has learned about you across conversations (themes, desired feelings, personality signals)
- **Recent Conversations** — your chat history
- **Script Library** — all generated experience scripts

### Script Library

Every script you save is stored in your Script Library. You can:

- View the full script
- Favourite scripts
- Archive scripts you are done with
- Share scripts (coming soon)

## What to Test

Here is what we would love your feedback on:

### 1. First Impression
- Does the welcome message feel warm and personal?
- Is LEXA's tone right — not too formal, not too casual?

### 2. Speed to Script
- Did you get a script within 2-3 messages?
- Did LEXA ask unnecessary questions, or did it get to the point?

### 3. Script Quality
- Does the hook create emotional recognition? ("They understand me")
- Are the signature highlights intriguing?
- Does the target profile feel accurate?
- Would you want to see the full day-by-day journey?

### 4. Relevance
- Did LEXA use what you said? (If you said "adrenaline", did the script reflect that?)
- Were the POIs/destinations relevant to your request?
- Did the buttons/quick replies match what was being discussed?

### 5. Preferences
- Was the preference form easy to use?
- Are the dropdown options comprehensive enough?
- Did you notice LEXA using your preferences in subsequent conversations?

### 6. Voice Input
- Did the push-to-talk microphone work?
- Was the transcription accurate?
- Did it feel natural?

### 7. Bugs / Issues
- Anything broken, confusing, or frustrating?
- Any error messages?
- Anything that loaded slowly or not at all?

## How to Give Feedback

Please share your feedback in any of these ways:

1. **In the app:** Click the bug report button (bottom-left corner) to report issues with screenshots
2. **By message:** Send your feedback directly to Christian
3. **Screenshot it:** If something looks wrong or confusing, take a screenshot

When reporting issues, please include:
- What you did (e.g., "I typed 'romantic weekend in Monaco'")
- What you expected
- What actually happened
- Your browser and device (e.g., Chrome on MacBook, Safari on iPhone)

## Known Limitations (Beta)

- **PDF download** is not yet available — the button is a placeholder
- **Share functionality** is coming soon
- **Day-by-day journey** (Stage 2) generates in the background after you save — it may take 30-60 seconds
- **POI data** is richest for the French Riviera and Monaco — other destinations may have fewer specific venue suggestions
- **Voice input** works best in Chrome and Edge — Safari support may vary
- **Mobile experience** works but is optimised for desktop
- The script engine relies on a database of experience arcs — if LEXA cannot find a matching arc, it falls back to a Claude-generated script (still good, but less structured)
